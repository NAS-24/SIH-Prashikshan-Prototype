The Seamless Internship Workflow

1. Discovery & Application

Students find opportunities, faculty approves, and applications are submitted through the system.

2. Selection & Placement

Industry partners review applicants, conduct interviews, and finalize placements digitally.

3. Monitoring & Verification

Students log activities, supervisors verify work, and faculty tracks progress in real-time.

4. Completion & Grading

Final reports are generated, evaluated, and academic credits are awarded for completion.
